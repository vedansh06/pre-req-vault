and vault PDA. Then deposit transfers SOL from the user into the vault.
The withdraw instruction transfers the requested amount from the vault
PDA back to the user. Finally, close returns remaining funds and closes
the vault accounts.

program as a cross-program invocation within the same transaction. The
registration PDA is initialized by the registration program, not
directly by my vault program.
